P| Standard O-Rings &rarr; Using the JIS B 2401 chart to spec your O-ringJIS B 2401 is the Japanese Industrial Standard for metric O-rings, and this chart organizes every size into four application series so you specify the right seal by function: P for dynamic (moving) service, G for static (fixed) seals, S for compact and special fits, and V for vacuum. Engineers and buyers searching for JIS B 2401 O-ring sizes, Japanese metric O-rings, or P / G / S / V series dimensions use it to read the inside diameter and cross-section by series and part number, match a standardized bore and groove, and confirm the seal on Japanese-built equipment before releasing a drawing or requesting a quote.
